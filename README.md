# Prompt Parkour: AI Wellness Quest

A browser-playable, blocky side-scrolling game about safe and responsible AI usage, effective prompting, deepfake detection, privacy, and digital wellbeing.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser.

Controls:

- Move: arrow keys or A/D
- Jump: Space, W, or Up
- Interact: E or Enter
- Mobile: on-screen touch controls

## Structure

The game has six worlds intended to play as a 25-35 minute classroom or workshop experience:

1. Prompt Plaza: build an effective AI prompt.
2. Source Springs: sort evidence by reliability.
3. Deepfake Drift: inspect generated video and audio clues.
4. Privacy Peak: redact risky personal data.
5. Focus Forest: build digital wellbeing habits and complete a calm rhythm challenge.
6. Creator Castle: final responsible-AI boss with fairness, evidence, and release safeguards.

Score is primarily based on completion time, with bonuses for sparks and penalties for hints or damage.

Facilitators can open a single lab directly for testing with `?lab=promptForge`, `?lab=sourceBridge`, `?lab=deepfakeLab`, `?lab=privacyVault`, `?lab=wellbeingFlow`, or `?lab=finalBoss`.
